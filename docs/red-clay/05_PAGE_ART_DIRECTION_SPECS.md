# Red Clay Coffee — Page Art-Direction Specifications

**Document version:** 1.1.0  
**Change note:** Round 1.1 strengthens Canyon's implementation authority for Shop, coffee PDP, and desktop navigation while preserving Red Clay identity and content architecture.  
**Purpose:** Figma-replacement direction for code-first composition. This document describes the intended visual result and responsive behavior without pretending to be pixel-perfect.

## 1. Homepage

**Creative objective:** Establish a coffee-first brand world, then move from product to place, publication, vessel, and retention. The page should feel like an authored issue, not seven interchangeable ecommerce sections.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon restraint/commerce 70%; Red Clay material/cultural identity 100%; Onyx confidence 10–15% in one or two desktop moments.  
**Primary references:** `REF-CANYON-COLLECTION-OPEN-01`, `REF-CANYON-CARD-HOVER-01`, `REF-CANYON-LIFESTYLE-FULLBLEED-01`, `REF-CANYON-JOURNAL-INDEX-01`.  
**Red Clay transformation:** Laterite/basalt/bone chapter rhythm, region-specific origin plates, tactile materials, early coffee merchandising, folio-number hints, stronger architectural masking.  
**Light/dark rhythm:** Bright or image-led hero → bright compact Continuum → calm product canvas → darker/mineral origin transition → bright Edition reading field → warm ritual plate → structured dark/bright footer. Avoid alternating mechanically.  
**Typography:** Hero serif carries brand proposition; grotesk handles utility; large section statements appear selectively.  
**Image dominance:** High at hero/origin/ritual; moderate in Harvest/Edition; low in Continuum/footer.  
**Content density:** Medium, with compact conceptual beats and open visual pauses.  
**Commerce:** Current Harvest in first third; restrained quick-add; Edition and Cup link into deeper paths.  
**Mobile:** One complete vertical narrative; products and primary CTA early; dedicated imagery; no desktop pins.  
**Desktop:** Asymmetric hero, editorial harvest rhythm, three spatial plates, one split Edition spread, large ritual image.  
**Motion:** Hero settling, Continuum progression, card swap, restrained masks, Edition chapter transition.  
**Assets:** All `HOME-*`.  
**Avoid:** Centered logo/headline/button hero, five equal Shopify cards, duplicate Folio, overlong manifesto, architecture that reads as hotel/property.

### HOME / HERO ENCOUNTER

**Intent:** Identify Red Clay as a coffee house immediately while establishing place, material, and early-light atmosphere.  
**Borrow:** Canyon’s visual calm and tiny UI over dominant photography.  
**Transform:** A Red Clay-specific highland/ritual composition with laterite/basalt/linen; coffee or Cup must be legible, not merely scenery.  
**Desktop:** Image and copy occupy unequal fields. Leave meaningful negative space for the serif proposition and two restrained links. Hero can approach viewport height but must reveal the next rhythm.  
**Mobile:** Dedicated `HOME-HERO-M`; copy and primary CTA remain within early view; no overlaid text on a busy focal area.  
**Motion:** Image settles 1–2% or a mask resolves after load; copy resolves in one controlled sequence; still is complete.  
**Avoid:** Autoplay film dependency, resort pavilion imagery, generic café, excessive type splitting.

### HOME / THE CONTINUUM

**Intent:** Explain the system in seconds.  
**Reference:** Publication rhythm, not a reference-site component.  
**Desktop:** Four-beat typographic strip with one quiet material field or rule system.  
**Mobile:** Four concise stacked beats; do not create swipe cards.  
**Motion:** Optional progress rule or sequential emphasis as the block enters; not four fade-ups.  
**Avoid:** Icons, diagrams, scientific causality, full-screen manifesto.

### HOME / CURRENT HARVEST

**Intent:** A curated seasonal release, not a generic ecommerce grid.  
**Reference:** `REF-CANYON-COLLECTION-OPEN-01`, `REF-CANYON-CARD-HOVER-01`.  
**Borrow:** Whitespace, image dominance, minimal chrome, alternate-image/quick-add restraint.  
**Transform:** One featured coffee creates hierarchy; supporting lots and Cup vary scale while sharing anatomy; include region, notes, concise metadata.  
**Desktop:** Stagger or span cards across the grid; one strong image ground, laterite/basalt details, generous row separation.  
**Mobile:** Featured coffee first, then three compact coffees and distinct compact Cup; visible actions; avoid five uninterrupted 3:4 towers.  
**Motion:** Image swap/clip on pointer; quick-add appears within image plane; instant/simplified reduced-motion.  
**Avoid:** Badges, ratings, filter controls, four-identical-column Shopify layout, bouncing/lifting cards.

### HOME / ORIGIN TERRITORIES

**Intent:** Introduce three distinct landscapes without teaching the full dossier.  
**Reference:** Canyon full-bleed confidence plus Section 5 region differentiation.  
**Desktop:** Three large plates with distinct rhythm: Kenya structured, Burundi vertical, Ethiopia open/botanical. Titles and short context sit with ample space.  
**Mobile:** Clear vertical sequence; optional snap only after testing and never required.  
**Motion:** Restrained plate masks and folio number cue; link to `/origins`.  
**Avoid:** Map, contour decoration, identical cards, fake elevation data.

### HOME / FEATURED EDITION

**Intent:** Prove “stories are storefronts” with one publication-grade chapter.  
**Reference:** `REF-CANYON-JOURNAL-INDEX-01`; selective Onyx confidence.  
**Desktop:** One large environmental plate and offset masthead/excerpt; product link is secondary but visible.  
**Mobile:** Image → volume → title → excerpt → article → coffee.  
**Motion:** Chapter rule/mask transition; no long pin by default.  
**Avoid:** Fake interview quotes, multiple article cards, full article duplication.

### HOME / KILN CUP & RITUAL

**Intent:** Close the continuum through one object and a believable brew ritual.  
**Reference:** `REF-CANYON-LIFESTYLE-FULLBLEED-01`.  
**Desktop:** Large ritual plate or object/material split; Cup remains companion to coffee.  
**Mobile:** Compact image and short description with `View the Kiln Cup`.  
**Motion:** Optional steam/light loop on eligible desktop; static poster everywhere else.  
**Avoid:** 3D mandate, technical performance claims, ceramics catalog feel.

## 2. Shop

**Creative objective:** A quiet seasonal release wall, not a product database. Make five products feel deliberately edited and immediately scannable.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon collection 85%; Red Clay product/material identity provides differentiation.  
**Primary references:** `REF-CANYON-COLLECTION-OPEN-01`, `REF-CANYON-COLLECTION-GRID-02`, `REF-CANYON-CARD-HOVER-01`.  
**Light/dark rhythm:** Primarily Bone/Linen for trust and product clarity; one restrained material/dispatch transition may use Basalt or Laterite.  
**Typography/image/density:** Large serif opening, image-dominant products, concise grotesk metadata, low UI density.  
**Commerce:** All five items visible; no filters; quick-add does not bypass required option choice.  
**Mobile:** One featured coffee followed by compact supporting products and a distinct Kiln Cup companion entry; natural single-column discovery.  
**Desktop:** Canyon-informed calm image-led product grid with substantial whitespace. Four coffees dominate; the Kiln Cup interrupts through a secondary row, different span, or distinct scale.  
**Motion:** Alternate-image swap and quick-add reveal only.  
**Assets:** `SHOP-*`.  
**Avoid:** Dense horizontal specification rows, four identical Shopify cards, filters, category tabs, ratings, discount badges, floating buttons, rounded card UI, or permanent button clutter.

### SHOP / COLLECTION OPENING

**Intent:** Signal a small, curated Volume 01.  
**Desktop:** Generous top field; H1 and one sentence occupy an asymmetric editorial position; first product imagery begins with breathing room.  
**Mobile:** Shorter opening so product appears early; H1 need not be viewport-sized.  
**Motion:** None beyond subtle page entry.  
**Avoid:** Promotional banner, filter bar, centered SaaS composition.

### SHOP / HARVEST LIST

**Intent:** Compare products through imagery, origin, notes, useful metadata, and status.  
**Desktop:** Four image-dominant coffee cards form the primary calm grid; use consistent grounds, concise metadata below imagery, minimal permanent actions, and one purposeful alternate image per product. Give the Kiln Cup a distinct secondary-row/span/scale treatment. Deliberate asymmetry must express hierarchy, not random bento styling.  
**Mobile:** Image, identity, notes, price/status, clear view/add action; alternate large/compact rhythm.  
**Motion:** Canyon-like image swap; accessible option sheet; no card movement.  
**Avoid:** Unverified metadata, forced grind default, decorative scarcity.

### SHOP / DISPATCH NOTE

**Intent:** Provide operational trust without inventing operations.  
**Desktop/mobile:** A concise typographic rule section with `[ROAST DAY]` and `[DISPATCH WINDOW]` placeholders hidden from public production until resolved.  
**Avoid:** Fake countdown, shipping promise, or transparent-trade claim.

## 3. Coffee PDP

**Creative objective:** Let a buyer decide immediately, then reveal coffee character and place with publication pacing.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon PDP 75%; Red Clay origin/material storytelling 100%.  
**Primary references:** `REF-CANYON-PDP-TOP-01`, `REF-CANYON-PDP-DETAIL-01`, `REF-CANYON-PDP-NARRATIVE-02`, `REF-CANYON-RELATED-01`.  
**Light/dark rhythm:** Bright purchase field → image/material or darker sensory statement → bright reading → full/wide origin/process plate → calm related products.  
**Typography:** Product title and sensory statement in serif; purchase choices and facts in grotesk; mono limited to provenance metadata.  
**Image dominance:** Very high at top and interludes; body copy remains readable, not overlaid.  
**Content density:** High only in Level 1 purchase module; editorial continuation breathes.  
**Commerce:** Level 1 complete; origin/Edition links; related items maximum three.  
**Mobile:** Buy module follows gallery immediately; sticky bar after intersection; metadata collapses.  
**Desktop:** Primary prototype is a three-zone opening—purchase/story, dominant product image, metadata/details—using unequal widths. The 58/42 composition remains an alternate prototype only. Both release into increasingly publication-like storytelling.  
**Motion:** Gallery change, sticky-state transition, image masks, related-card swap.  
**Assets:** Relevant `PDP-*` family.  
**Avoid:** Technical dashboard, star ratings, full-page sticky purchase panel, deterministic flavor claims, repeated product copy.

### PDP / HERO + PURCHASE

**Intent:** Complete Level 1 without reading the story.  
**Desktop:** Primary prototype: Zone A resolves purchase identity, tasting, price, and options; Zone B carries the dominant product image; Zone C holds restrained origin, process, dispatch, and technical context. Do not force equal thirds. A bounded sticky relationship may be tested, but it releases before the editorial continuation. Alternate prototype: the earlier 58/42 media/purchase composition. Browser review selects the production direction.  
**Mobile:** Gallery → name/region/notes → price/options → Add to Bag → dispatch. Main CTA full-width; sticky bar waits until this module exits.  
**Motion:** Crossfade/clip between images, no spinning product.  
**Avoid:** Hidden price, hover-only variants, preselected misleading grind, purchase panel pinning into footer.

### PDP / PRODUCT CHARACTER

**Intent:** Turn sensory direction into one memorable editorial beat.  
**Desktop:** Large serif statement with concise supporting paragraph; optional material/detail plate.  
**Mobile:** Statement scales down and wraps naturally; copy remains inside gutter.  
**Motion:** Single rule or mask transition; no word-by-word gimmick.  
**Avoid:** Ratings/testimonials copied from Canyon, claims that process or soil “creates” a note.

### PDP / PLACE & PROCESS

**Intent:** Provide enough verified regional context to make the coffee meaningful and lead to the dossier.  
**Desktop:** Alternate narrow text and large image plates; one asymmetrical split may pin briefly.  
**Mobile:** Short chapters with full-width image break.  
**Motion:** Restrained plate reveal.  
**Avoid:** Fake station, named producer, universal processing duration, duplicated dossier.

### PDP / EDITION + METADATA

**Intent:** Offer deeper reading and optional factual detail without cluttering purchase.  
**Desktop:** Edition card has editorial prominence; metadata sits as a fine-rule table.  
**Mobile:** Edition first; native-style disclosure for specifications.  
**Avoid:** Dense dashboard, unverified fields rendered to fill space.

### PDP / RELATED PRODUCTS

**Intent:** Continue curation, not pressure.  
**Reference:** `REF-CANYON-RELATED-01`.  
**Desktop:** Up to three related products with same card system.  
**Mobile:** Compact two-column or horizontal-free list depending text length.  
**Avoid:** Discount assumptions, endless carousel, unrelated bestsellers.

## 5. Origins Hub

**Creative objective:** Deliver the site’s signature authored moment, then return to clear educational and commerce paths.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon restraint at rest; Red Clay-specific Section 5 motion; TOAST/Kinfolk publication rhythm as supporting context.  
**Primary references:** Section 5, `REF-CANYON-LIFESTYLE-FULLBLEED-01`, `REF-CANYON-PDP-NARRATIVE-02`.  
**Rhythm:** Quiet bright thesis → immersive Folio that may move through Bone/Basalt/Laterite planes → calm bright educational continuation → product/Edition bridges.  
**Typography:** Oversized region names counterbalance photography; metadata is quiet and aligned; folio numbers/rules provide continuity.  
**Image dominance:** Highest on the site within the Folio, then deliberately lowers.  
**Content density:** Sparse Folio; moderate education/commerce after release.  
**Mobile:** Pocket monograph; no cinematic pin.  
**Desktop:** Bounded sticky stage and distinct chapter rhythms.  
**Motion:** Section 5 only; rest of page reverts to quieter global system.  
**Assets:** `ORIGINS-*`, `S5-FOLIO-*`.  
**Avoid:** Map, cards inside pinned stage, fake terrain, scroll-jacking, long chapter prose, cloned region layouts.

### ORIGINS / THESIS + APPROACH

**Intent:** Orient the user before motion begins.  
**Desktop:** H1 and concise thesis in normal flow; direct region index and first image edge invite entry.  
**Mobile:** Same thesis, then first complete chapter.  
**Motion:** Nothing fires until Folio nears viewport.  
**Avoid:** Auto-starting reveal above the fold, technical explanation of interaction.

### ORIGINS / EARTHEN FOLIO

**Intent:** Make origin presentation feel like moving through a designed publication.  
**Desktop:** Photography dominates; region title is counterweight; metadata and CTA remain compact; region nav is directly actionable. Kenya is precise/lateral, Burundi vertical/layered, Ethiopia broad/botanical.  
**Mobile:** Three stacked chapters with dedicated images, Place/Process/Cup, and dossier links.  
**Motion:** Architectural masks, offset timing, material planes, clear entry/release; governed by `08_SIGNATURE_EXPERIENCE_SPEC.md`.  
**Avoid:** slideshow, fake book, parallax gallery, 3D, map, invisible dossier links.

### ORIGINS / REGIONAL CONTINUATION

**Intent:** Preserve direct region discovery if the Folio’s compact chapter view is not sufficient.  
**Desktop/mobile:** Scaffold compact entries, visually quieter than Folio. Consolidate only after prototype review.  
**Avoid:** Repeating Folio at full scale.

### ORIGINS / HOW TO READ AN ORIGIN

**Intent:** Explain Place, Variety, Process, Cup without a deterministic formula.  
**Desktop:** Four concise terms aligned by fine rules or typographic columns.  
**Mobile:** Simple vertical definition list.  
**Motion:** None or minimal rule resolution.  
**Avoid:** Icons, equations, charts, fake data.

### ORIGINS / COFFEES + EDITIONS

**Intent:** Convert context into product and deeper reading.  
**Desktop:** Compact, curated bridges; products first, Editions second.  
**Mobile:** Clear cards/list; no carousel dependency.  
**Avoid:** Repeating full Shop or Journal.

## 10. Header / Menu

**Creative objective:** Nearly disappear until needed while making Shop, Origins, Editions, About, and Bag obvious.  
**References:** `REF-CANYON-NAV-MEGA-01`, with selective confidence from `REF-ONYX-NAV-PROMO-01`.  
**Transformation:** Prototype a restrained Canyon-derived **editorial reveal menu**, not a conventional ecommerce mega-menu. Use mineral surfaces, restrained active rules, short link groups, and at most one contextual image/feature.  
**Mobile:** 48 px header; full-screen typographic menu; body lock and focus trap.  
**Desktop:** 72 px quiet nav; pinned/smart-hide remains a prototype decision. Shop reveal may group four coffees, the Kiln Cup, and one current-harvest/Edition feature. Origins reveal may list three regions and one contextual origin feature. Keep only if browser testing improves discovery without inflating the catalog.  
**Motion:** Fast panel height/clip/opacity transition; links resolve as one composition, not a staggered spectacle.  
**Assets:** At most one current-harvest/Edition feature for Shop and one contextual origin feature for Origins; reuse approved families where possible.  
**Avoid:** Giant ecommerce mega-menu, multi-level taxonomy, category overload, promo wall, glass blur dependency, or inaccessible hover-only opening.

## 11. Cart

**Creative objective:** Resolve purchase quickly and predictably.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon ecommerce restraint; no need for editorial spectacle.  
**Rhythm:** Basalt/Obsidian or Bone drawer with clear line items, fine rules, persistent subtotal/checkout action.  
**Typography/density:** Grotesk utility; compact but not cramped.  
**Commerce:** One upsell maximum; preserve contents on recoverable error; concluded-item handling; no discount unless approved.  
**Mobile:** Near-full-height bottom sheet, safe-area padding, visible close.  
**Desktop:** 420 px right drawer and subdued backdrop.  
**Motion:** Fast translate + backdrop opacity; instant reduced-motion fallback.  
**Assets:** Product thumbnails, optional `GLOBAL-EMPTY-BAG`.  
**Avoid:** Cross-sell carousel, articles, confetti, fake shipping threshold, blocked close, swipe-only dismissal.

## 12. Footer

**Creative objective:** End with a clear directory, Dispatch sign-up, and honest utility placeholders.  
**Reference mix:** Canyon’s minimal UI chrome and publication spacing.  
**Rhythm:** Architectural rules, four desktop groups, stacked mobile groups; may use dark Basalt close or bright Bone based on preceding section.  
**Mobile:** No deep accordions at launch.  
**Desktop:** Four columns with uneven but balanced content weights.  
**Motion:** None beyond link focus/hover.  
**Assets:** `GLOBAL-FOOTER-TYPO`, optional newsletter/legal marks.  
**Avoid:** Unsupported sourcing/impact copy, social feed, excessive legal teaser, giant newsletter takeover.
